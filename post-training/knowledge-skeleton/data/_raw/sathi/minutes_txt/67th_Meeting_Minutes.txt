Minutes of the 67th Meeting of Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards,
Notification and Release of Varieties for Agricultural Crops held under the
chairmanship of Prof. S. K. Datta, Deputy Director General (Crop Science), ICAR
on 13th November, 2013 at Committee Hall, ICAR, Krishi Bhavan, New Delhi.
The list of participants is at Annexure-I.
The Chairman, Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards, Notification and Release of
Varieties for Agricultural Crops welcomed the members of the Sub-Committee and special
invitees for the meeting. He commended that the Secretariat of the Sub-Committee had timely
issued the minutes of 66th Meeting of the Sub-Committee. Joint Secretary (Seeds) mentioned
that, the Sub-Committee may consider only superior varieties with higher yield parameters
including resistant to pests and diseases. He further stated that, seed production of susceptible
varieties should not be taken up in the areas which are endemic for attack of pests and diseases.
He specifically cited the example of two Wheat varieties namely HD 2932 and HD 2977 which
are susceptible to rust disease and are being sold in Punjab State which is not recommended for
cultivation of these varieties. He therefore suggested that seed production and cultivation of
these two varieties should be stopped in the North West Plain Zone including Haryana, Punjab,
Western Uttar Pradesh, Jammu etc. which are not recommended for cultivation of these
varieties. Joint Secretary (Seeds) also mentioned that Department of Agriculture and
Cooperation has advised the Public Sector Seed Corporations to disclose the expected
performance along with package of practices from Rabi 2013-14. Therefore, all
breeders/sponsoring institutions are advised to provide variety wise package of practice along
with the Release/Notification proposal to be submitted to the Sub-Committee. Without package
of practices for cultivation of variety, the proposal for Release/Notification should not be
considered. The Deputy Commissioner(QC) and Member Secretary of the Sub-Committee
mentioned that the specific action had been taken on deferred proposals of previous meetings in
consultation with the concerned Breeder / PC / PD and after removing various deficiencies the
same have been included in the Agenda Note for this meeting. Thereafter, the Member-
Secretary of the Sub-Committee presented the Agenda items for consideration by the Sub-
Committee.

Item No. 5: Dispute of parentage between Maize c.v. Kaveri 50 (KMH 50) of Kaveri Seeds
and 30V92 of Pioneer Seeds.
This matter was earlier referred to PPV & FR Authority for indicating distinctiveness in
the above two varieties in DUS test and registration under PPV & FR Act, 2001. Now, the PPV
and FR Authority has informed that registration of KMH 50 cannot be done as the same has
been stayed by the Hon’ble Delhi High Court in W.P.(C) No.6819/2011. Sub-Committee again
deferred the proposal for want of clear and unambiguous comments regarding registration of
these varieties by PPV&FR Authority.

(iii) The guidelines for suggesting uniform system for denomination of Central and State
varieties.
(iv) The check list and procedure for submitting proposal to the Member Secretary of Sub-
Committee through hard copy and soft copy.
(v) The proforma for submitting the proposal for State varieties for notification should also
be revised in line with proforma for release/ notification of central varieties.
The meeting ended with a vote of thanks to the Chairman, members and special invitee
of the committee.
