Minutes of the 61st Meeting of Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards, Notification
and Release of Varieties for Agricultural Crops held under the Chairmanship of
Prof. S.K. Datta, Deputy Director General (Crop Sciences), ICAR on 15th October, 2011 at
Karnataka State Seed Corporation, Bangalore.
The Chairman, Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards, Notification and Release of
Varieties for Agricultural Crops welcomed members of the Sub-Committee and special invitees.
The list of the participants who attended the meeting is annexed as Annexure-I.
Member-Secretary of the Sub-Committee provided the account of the earlier Sub-
Committee and its follow-up action and the issues to be discussed by this Sub-Committee.
After introduction, regular agenda items were taken-up for discussion as under:-
Item No.1: Confirmation of the minutes of 60th meeting of Central Sub-Committee on
Crop Standards, Notification and Release of Varieties for Agricultural
Crops held on 28th June, 2011 at NBPGR, New Delhi.
The minutes of 60th meeting of Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards,
Notification and Release of Varieties for Agricultural Crops has been circulated vide letter No.
3-10/2010-SD.IV dated 23rd September, 2011. No comments have been received on the
minutes of the meeting; therefore, the minutes of the meeting were confirmed. The Sub-
Committee noted that the Gazette Notification for the varieties recommended for notification
has been issued vide S.O. No. 2326(E) dated 10th October, 2011.

Item No. 5: Renaming of paddy cultivar CSR 30 (88-H5-1-1-2) (IET 14720) notified vide
SO No. 1134(E) dated 15.11.2001 as Basmati CSR30.
The issue under reference has been discussed in the meeting. The committee
noted that the paddy cultivar CSR30 has been notified vide SO No. 1134(E) dated 15.11.2001
The proposal in question has already been discussed in the 60th meeting of the Central Sub-
Committee on Crop Standard Notification and Release of Variety on Agricultural Crops for
renaming the variety as Basmati. Accordingly, it was recommended to rename the variety. This
recommendation was put up to Chairman, Central seed committee for his approval. The
competent authority has sought clarification whether the data generated by the sponsoring
authority under the National Basmati Trials where on sodic soil or on normal soil and what has
been its performance including expression of the Basmati characters in the light of the fact that
CSR30 was first recommended for release and cultivation for the areas of North Western states
of UP, Haryana and Punjab for sodic soil (pH =8.8-9.5) and notified in the year 2001 as an
2
ordinary rice. Hence, this vital information along with the relevant position in respect of
Geographical Indication for declaring this variety as Basmati needs to be confirmed. While
declaring the variety under reference as Basmati it needs to be checked whether it would
amount to dilution of India’s position vis-à-vis other Basmati producers.
Meeting ended with a vote of thanks to the Chair.
