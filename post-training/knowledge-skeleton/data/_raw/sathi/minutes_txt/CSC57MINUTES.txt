Notification and Release of Varieties for Agricultural Crops held under the

Item No. 7: Drafting the content of Notification.
Additional Secretary (AB), DAC while reviewing the content of the notification being
issued by the DAC pointed out that “the need for Central notification for seed varieties released
by the State needs to be revisited. It seems that the only purpose of Central notification is to
allow the released seed to be produced in other States (for which one year trials are conducted
under AICRP for collecting evidence to ensure that the seed is resistant to pests and diseases
in the States where it is to be produced). We do not, apparently, evaluate the performance of
such seed on the grounds that this has already been done by the State Committee.
Furthermore, the wording of the Central notification for release of seed varieties is misleading
and seems to suggest that the impugned varieties are recommended for cultivation throughout
the country. We should, in the notification, make it clear that the varieties are being released
on the advice of the State Seed Committee (on evaluation through multi-locational trials) and
are recommended for cultivation only in the State of their release but whose seeds can be
produced in any other State as well. This will prevent the seeds from being sown in areas
which are not suitable for their cultivation.”
The views of the AS (AB) have been taken into consideration with regards to drafting
the notification. Accordingly, following notification has been suggested in consultation with
DAC so as, to convey the proper meaning and purpose of the notification.
S.O. ____________ In exercise of the powers conferred by section 5 of the
Seeds Act, 1966 (54 of 1966), the Central Government, after consultation with the
Central Seed Committee, being of the opinion that it is necessary and expedient to
regulate the quality of the seeds of the varieties specified in column (3) of the
Table below of the kinds specified in the corresponding entries in column (2) of the
said Table, to be sold for purposes of agriculture, hereby declares that the said
varieties of seeds shall be the notified varieties for the whole of India for purposes
of seed production and quality control and the said varieties are further
recommended for release and cultivation in States specified in column (4) of the
Table.

Item No. 8 Notification of Paddy variety Sahbhagi dhan.
The Paddy variety Sahbhagi Dhan (IET No.19576) IR 74371-70-1-1-CRR-1) was
deferred in the 54th Sub-committee meeting, for want of clarification on the authorship and
parentage details and the Sponsoring Authority was advised to re-submit the revised proposal
along with the required information for consideration.
The Sponsoring Authority re-submitted the proposal without providing the required
details regarding authorship and parentage. In fact they have submitted the same proposal,
which was submitted to the 54th Sub-committee.
The Committee noted that the sponsoring authority has not made any efforts to
clarify lacuna which has been pointed out by the 54th Sub-committee. Hence, the Committee
directed to the sponsoring authority to look into the decision of the 54th Sub-committee and to
clarify the position. The Committee further would also like to know what intervention was
made in the proposal.
Besides to the above, the Committee took the following decision:-
1. Breeders/sponsoring authority should prepare themselves to provide the information
with regards to DNA Marker/Finger Printing, prime number, etc. of the variety
proposed for notification from 1st January, 2011 onwards.
2. While submitting the proposal, they should provide lucid photograph of the
field, individual plant, cob, rachis, grain, etc. so as to consider physical appearance
the cultivar.
