Item No.5: Review of Handbook of seed and planting material testing manual and
labels for horticultural crops.
The Chairman, Central Sub-committee on Crops Standards, Notification and Release of
Varieties for Horticultural Crops-cum- Deputy Director General(Horticulture), ICAR directed the
concerned Project Coordinators to study the following issues and to prepare the draft material for
Seed and Planting Material Testing Manual within three months.
1. Seeds/planting material sampling.
2. Receipt and registration of seeds/planting material samples.
3. Physical purity analysis for seeds/planting material.
4. Germination testing procedure for seeds/planting material.
5. Determination of gen-uinenessof cultivars of seeds/planting material.
6. Moisture determination (where ever applicable).
7. Determination of the seeds/planting material health.
8. Testing of seeds/planting material vigour.
9. Storage of seeds/planting material.
10. Any other test required such as polycarylamide Gel Electrophoresis, etc.
11. Suggested list of equipments for testing seeds/planting material.
12. Forms used in seeds/planting testing laboratories:-
a. Seeds and planting material Analysis Card.
b. Seeds and planting material Analysis Report.
c. Seeds and planting material Analysis Report for Revalidation Lot.
13. Recommended list of Books and Journals for seeds/planting material testing.
14. Test for Heterogeneity.
