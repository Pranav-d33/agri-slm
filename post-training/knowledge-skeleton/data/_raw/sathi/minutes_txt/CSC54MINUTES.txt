Item No. 5: Registration of varieties which have been recommended for release/notification are to
be registered simultaneously with the PVP Authority.
The Committee noted that the breeder who develops the variety has not take any initiative for
preparing the documents and not following the relevant procedures to register the variety with the PVP
Authority so as to protect the public variety. After the in-depth discussion with regards to time to be taken
for preparing the documents to register with the PVP and to submit the proposal for release and
notification, it was decided that whenever the variety entered into AVT 1, the breeder simultaneously has
to prepare the document for registering the public variety with the PVP. Sub-Committee advised that the
breeder concerned to expedite the procedure along with the AVT trials in advance so that registration
process with PVP will be started. It was also resolved that the breeder concerned should take initiative to
register hybrid as well parent lines with PVP. Whenever the proposal to be submitted by the sponsoring
authority to the Sub-Committee for release/notification, they should indicate in the proposal about the
initiative taken to register the variety with the PVP in future.
Item No.6: Protocol/procedure to be developed for testing the Bt. Cotton BGII, fused gene and
other events:
The Committee noted that Government of Andhra Pradesh, Department of Agriculture informed
vide their letter No.SRC (4) 124/07 dated 23.09.09 that seed stocks of Bt. cotton BGII are being sold in
the State of Andhra Pradesh large quantities to the extent of about 40 lakhs packets every year. However,
there is no procedure available to regulate the quality of such seeds by way of sampling and analyzing the
same in the notified seed testing laboratories. In the meeting, Department of Seed Certification, Govt. of
Tamil Nadu has provided the basic material for testing procedure for Cry 1 Ac/BG1, ELISA method for
Cry 1 Ac, Cry 2 Ab.BGII and ELISA method for BGII Cry 1 Ac and Cry 1 Ab and fused gene (Cry 1 Ac
& Cry 1 Ab)-ELISA method for the perusal of the Committee. The Chairman of the Sub-Committee
perused the basic material and discussed with the Director, Central Cotton Research Institute, Nagpur over
phone about this issue. The Chairman of the sub-Committee directed the Director, Central Cotton
Research Institute, Nagpur to provide the required procedure to test the Bt. cotton BGI, BGII and fused
gene and other events, if any, to the Sub-Committee so as to circulate to all the state seed testing
laboratories and to follow the procedure for testing the transgenic gene at the earliest. It was also decided
that till that time, the procedure given by Department of Seed certification, Govt. of Tamil Nadu may be
kept in abeyance and not to circulate as it requires some more improvement.

Item no.7: List of crop/varieties to be offered for OECD Seed Scheme.
The Committee noted that India became the OECD member for OECD seed scheme during
October, 2008. As to comply the requirement with regards to updating the annul list of crop/varieties and
parental constituents of OECD, a Workshop has been convened during the month of August, 2009 in
which list of varieties to be provided to the OECD has been prepared. Joint Secretary (Seeds), who is the
National Designated Authority for OECD desired that the list identified in the Workshop has to be brought
to the notice of the Sub-committee before sending to the OECD. Accordingly, the list of varieties
identified in the Workshop has been circulated to the all members of the Committee for perusal.
The Committee studied this issue in depth and decided that the Project Coordinator of the
concerned crops are to be discussed and to get their view on what basis these crops/varieties are to be
approved. The issue related to parentage, registration with PVP, the impact on account of providing the
list of varieties to other foreign countries in the absence of registration with the PVP, etc. are to be studied
in detail. Taking into consideration of the above, the Committee desired that a formal letter listing out
varieties to be sent to the OECD for Varietal Certification Scheme may be forwarded to the Director
General, ICAR from the level of the Joint Secretary (Seeds) for comments and approval as the issue under
reference is out of the purview of the Sub-committee.
Item no.8: Notification of Wheat variety Pusa Borlaug (HD 2967).
During the discussion, with the approval of the Chairman, Head, Seeds Science and Technology,
IARI, Pusa, New Delhi, who is the member of the Sub-committee stated that IARI developed the Wheat
variety named as Pusa Borlaug (HD 2967) has not been listed for consideration in this meeting as the
variety to be named after Borlaug to honor the Nobel price winner and the service rendered by him to
Indian farmers. It was also stated by the Head, SS&T, IARI that a World Conference is going to be held in
the month of November, 2009 in which Hon’ble Minister of Agriculture, Government of India, many
prominent world famous scientists and administrators are expected to attend the meeting. Therefore, she
proposed that the variety to be considered for notification to salute Dr. Borlaug for his service rendered to
Indian farmers and this has to be announced in the World Conference. Since the proposal has not been
received for consideration, the Chairman directed the Head, Seeds Science and Technology, IARI, Pusa,
New Delhi to get the proposal on fax.
12
Accordingly, the proposal has been received by fax and studied the proposal in depth in
consultation with the experts. The experts stated that the variety under reference is not performing upto
the mark compares to the existing verities DWR-17. The yield advantage is not at all superior over the
check variety and also it is susceptible to diseases. The Committee comes to the conclusion that the IARI
Wheat variety Pusa HD 2967 is not at all eligible to name after Dr. Borlaug. Therefore, it is suggested that
the best variety i.e. DWR-17 has to be re-named as Pusa Borlaug(DWR-17). The Chairman, Sub-
committee directed the Head, SS&T to initiate necessary action to re-name DWR-17 as Pusa
Borlaug(DWR-17) to mark the respect to Dr. Borlaug in consultation with the Director General, ICAR
and subsequently Gazette Notification for amendment to re-name the variety will be issued.
Meeting ended with vote of thanks to the Chair.
