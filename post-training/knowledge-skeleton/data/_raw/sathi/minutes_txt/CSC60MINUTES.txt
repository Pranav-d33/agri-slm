Item No. 6: Proposal to review variety of wheat HD2967 submitted by IARI New Delhi for
release/notification.
The Committee noted that the proposal under reference has been discussed by the
subcommittee in its 54th, 56th, 57th & 58th meetings and no final decision for release/notification as
Pusa Borlaug (HD 2967). Committee further noted that a special meeting was convened under
the chairmanship of the Secretary (A&C) on 29th March 2011, on the request of sponsoring
authority to reconsider this proposal. In the meeting it was decided that the matter be placed
before the Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards, Notification and Release of Varieties for
Agricultural Crops for review along with updated data if any, pertaining to the variety HD-2967 in
addition to yield data and data on evidence of resistance to pest and diseases etc. which was
available in 2009 for 3 years. Further the Sub- Committee may examine whether earlier 3 years
data relating to the variety was interpreted appropriately.
In response to the decision, the sponsoring authority submitted the following
information.
“The yield performance was compared after 3 and 4 year, the new variety HD 2967 showed
slightly higher yield than the checks.
Disease Reaction:
The HD 2967 was scored as 40S with ACI 11.2 as compared to 10S with ACI 3.0 of
DBW 17 for stripe rust in 2006-07 and showed susceptibility to reaction to some of the races of
brown and yellow rust, thus considered susceptible to diseases as per the original proposal.

Chairman, Central Seed Committee reviewed the issue with AS (GCP), DDG(CS),
ICAR and JS(Seeds) on 08.09.2011. It was noted from the data furnished by ICAR from 2006-07
to 2010-11 on the disease reaction of HD-2967 to yellow rust, that in the year 2010-11, HD-2967
has a highest score (HS) of 10 on the susceptibility scale which is 1/8th of the PBW-343 and
DBW-17, and 1/6th of PBW-550. In respect of brown rust, for the same year i.e. 2010-11, HD-2967
has a highest score (HS) of 5 on the susceptibility scale which is 1/12th of PBW-343 but equal to
PBW-550. However, DBW-17 is moderately susceptible during 2010-11 and hence, on
comparative basis, HD-2967 fares lower in disease reaction to brown rust viz-a-viz. DBW-17.
From 2006-07 to 2010-11, disease reaction of HD-2967 and other varieties to yellow rust and
brown rust has shown variation from moderately resistant to moderately susceptible to susceptible
but the variation are totally inconsistent and do not display any particular pattern across the years
for any co-relation against a common denominator. These variations are attributable to
environmental conditions, existing at that particular time, which directly impacts the onset and
intensity of rust disease and since these conditions never follow a fixed pattern, but varies
significantly from year to year, the co-relation of data across the years on a common denominator
is difficult. It was informed that, in the North West Plain Zone, it is yellow rust which is pre-
dominant and HD-2967’s performance against yellow rust as per above data is comparatively
better than other varieties. HD-2967 has also shown presence of minor genes in addition to major
genes and due to this genetic variability it tends to have an enhanced resistance to yellow rust.
In the light of the above and based on the available data of ICAR along with their comments
and also in view of the recommendations of Central sub- Committee on Crop Standards,
Notification and Release of Varieties for Agriculture Crops, Chairman Central Seed Committee
approved the recommendations for release/notification of the variety HD 2967 for the North West
Plain Zone (NWPZ) i.e Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan, Delhi, Western Uttar Pradesh (except Jhansi
Division), Jammu & Kathua region of J & K, Una district and Paonta Valley areas of HP, Tarai
region of Uttarakhand.
