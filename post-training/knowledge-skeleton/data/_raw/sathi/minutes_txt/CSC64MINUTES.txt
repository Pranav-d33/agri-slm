Minutes of the 64th Meeting of Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards, Notification
and Release of Varieties for Agriculture Crops held under the chairmanship of Prof. S.
K. Datta, Deputy Director General (Crop Science), ICAR Krishi Bhawan on 3rd
November, 2012 at Committee Hall of NBPGR, Pusa, New Delhi-110012.
The list of participants is at Annexure-I.
The Chairman, Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards, Notification and Release
of Varieties for Agricultural Crops welcomed the members of the Sub-Committee and special
invitees for the meeting. He commended that the Secretariat of the Sub-Committee had
timely issued the Minutes of 63rd Meeting of the Sub-Committee and also uploaded the same
on the website of DAC. The Deputy Commissioner(QC) and Member Secretary of the Sub-
Committee mentioned that the specific action had been taken on deferred proposals of
previous meetings in consultation with the concerned Breeder / PC / PD and after removing
various deficiencies the same have been included in the Agenda Note for this meeting.
Thereafter, the Member-Secretary of the Sub-Committee presented the Agenda items for
consideration by the Sub-Committee.
Item No.1: Confirmation of the minutes of 63rd meeting of Central Sub-Committee on
Crop Standards, Notification and Release of Varieties for Agricultural
Crops held on 28th – 29th June, 2012.
The minutes of 63rd meeting of Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards,
Notification and Release of Varieties for Agricultural Crops were confirmed. The Sub-
Committee noted that the Gazette Notification S.O. No. 2125(E) dated 10th September, 2012
was issued for the cultivars recommended in the 63rd Meeting of the Sub-Committee for
release/notification.

Item No. 5 : Amendment in the Gazette Notifications
63rd Meeting of the sub-committee recommends two proposals of Pearl Millet
(Hybrid) Bio 448 (MH 1671) for release/notification for two different zones i.e. Zone 1
(Rajasthan, Gujarat, Haryana, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Punjab and Delhi) and Zone 2
(Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu). However, in the notification
S.O. No. 2125 (E) dated 10.09.2012 only Rajasthan, Gujarat, Haryana, Madhya Pradesh,
Uttar Pradesh, Punjab and Delhi has been included for cultivation of Pearl Millet (Hybrid)
Bio 448 (MH 1671). Now, the Committee recommends including Maharashtra, Karnataka,
Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu States for cultivation of above said Pearl Millet (Hybrid).
After considering specific proposals, general discussions were held and following
decisions were taken:
(1) Many proposals which were deferred in earlier meetings of central sub-committee for
want of specific information had not been resubmitted by the concerned breeder/
institutions. The concerned Project Director / Project Coordinator will take up this
matter with the defaulting institutions/ breeder for resubmission of such proposals after
removing various defects / deficiencies.
(2) ADG (Seeds), ICAR shall coordinate with the Director, DRR and Director, DMR for
revision of criteria and guidelines for release of new varieties. In the revised guidelines
the criteria for denomination of new varieties, testing procedure at various location and
testing of entries from international institutions etc. will also be covered.
(3) In the revised guidelines, the procedure for testing for disease/pest resistance/tolerance
under artificial inoculation and under natural field conditions should be included. The
method for recording data under biotic and abiotic stress conditions should also be
covered in the revised guidelines.
The next meeting of the Sub-Committee may be held in December, 2012 and the venue
for the meeting will be decided by the Chairman of the Sub-Committee in consultation with
the Member Secretary.
The meeting ended with the vote of thanks to the Chairman, members and special invitee
of the committee.
