Minutes of the 66th Meeting of Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards, Notification
and Release of Varieties for Agriculture Crops held under the chairmanship of Prof. S. K.
Datta, Deputy Director General (Crop Science), ICAR on 29th and 30th July, 2013 at
Committee Hall, Directorate of Maize Research, Pusa, New Delhi-110012.
The list of participants is at Annexure-I.
The Chairman, Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards, Notification and Release of
Varieties for Agricultural Crops welcomed the members of the Sub-Committee and special
invitees for the meeting. He commended that the Secretariat of the Sub-Committee had timely
issued the Minutes of 65th Meeting of the Sub-Committee. The Deputy Commissioner(QC) and
Member Secretary of the Sub-Committee mentioned that the specific action had been taken on
deferred proposals of previous meetings in consultation with the concerned Breeder / PC / PD
and after removing various deficiencies the same have been included in the Agenda Note for
this meeting. Thereafter, the Member-Secretary of the Sub-Committee presented the Agenda
items for consideration by the Sub-Committee.
Item No.1: Confirmation of the minutes of 65th meeting of Central Sub-Committee on
Crop Standards, Notification and Release of Varieties for Agricultural Crops
held on 18th January, 2013.
The minutes of 65th meeting of Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards, Notification
and Release of Varieties for Agricultural Crops were confirmed. The Sub-Committee noted that
the Gazette Notification S.O. No. 952(E) dated 10th April, 2013 was issued for the cultivars
recommended in the 65th Meeting of the Sub-Committee for release/notification.

Item No. 5: Furnishing the package of practice of cultivar
Department of Agriculture and Cooperation has advised NSC, SFCI and State Seeds
Corporations to disclose the expected performance along with details of yield in the seed packet
and on the labels from Rabi 2013. All breeders/sponsoring institutions are advised to provide
variety wise package of practice along with the Release/Notification proposal to be submitted to
the Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards, Notification and Release of varieties for
Agricultural Crops. Without package of practices for cultivation of variety, the proposal for
Release/Notification will not be considered by the Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards,
Notification and Release of varieties for Agricultural Crops.
