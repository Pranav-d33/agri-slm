Minutes of the 65th Meeting of Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards, Notification
and Release of Varieties for Agriculture Crops held under the chairmanship of Prof. S.
K. Datta, Deputy Director General (Crop Science), ICAR, Krishi Bhawan on 18th
January, 2013 at Central Institute for Cotton Research (CICR), Nagpur.
The list of participants is at Annexure-I.
The Chairman, Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards, Notification and Release
of Varieties for Agricultural Crops welcomed the members of the Sub-Committee and special
invitees for the meeting. He commended that the Secretariat of the Sub-Committee had
timely issued the Minutes of 64th Meeting of the Sub-Committee and also uploaded the same
on the website of DAC. The Deputy Commissioner(QC) and Member Secretary of the Sub-
Committee mentioned that the specific action had been taken on deferred proposals of
previous meetings in consultation with the concerned Breeder / PC / PD and after removing
various deficiencies the same have been included in the Agenda Note for this meeting.
Thereafter, the Member-Secretary of the Sub-Committee presented the Agenda items for
consideration by the Sub-Committee.
Item No.1: Confirmation of the minutes of 64th meeting of Central Sub-Committee on
Crop Standards, Notification and Release of Varieties for Agricultural
Crops held on 3rd November, 2012 at NBPGR, New Delhi.
The minutes of 64th meeting of Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards,
Notification and Release of Varieties for Agricultural Crops were circulated on 19th December
2012. No comments have been received on the minutes therefore, the same may be confirmed.
