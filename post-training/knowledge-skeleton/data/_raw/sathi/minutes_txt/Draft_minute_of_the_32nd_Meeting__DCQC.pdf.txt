Draft minute of the 32nd meeting of Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards, Notification
and Release of Varieties for Horticultural Crops held on 4th April, 2025 under the
Chairmanship of Prof. Sanjay Kumar Singh, Deputy Director General (Horticultural Science),
ICAR, New Delhi.
The list of participants is enclosed as Annexure-1.
At the outset, Dr. Dilip Kr. Srivastava, Member- Secretary welcomed Prof. Sanjay Kumar
Singh, Deputy Director General (Horticultural Sci.) cum Chairman of Central Sub-Committee on
Crop Standards, Notification for Horticulture Crops and embers and all the participants of
meeting. The Chairman also welcomed the participants and emphasized the importance of the
varieties of horticultural crops to put them in seed production chain to ensure the availability of
quality Seed of the Horticultural Crops among farmers. These varieties could be made available for
seed certification system after notification.
Dr. Dilip Kr. Srivastava, Dy. Comm. (QC), and Member- Secretary informed that the
Committee has been mandated with statutory responsibility of release and notification of varieties for
all India level also for notification of the state varieties. After general discussion, the regular agenda
was taken up for consideration of the Committee.
